About Martial Pilgrims Ministry

A people set apart, walking together in faith.

Discover who we are, what we believe, and the people God has raised to shepherd this ministry.

Our Mission

Martial Pilgrims Ministry exists to draw people to salvation in Jesus Christ, minister healing to the sick, deliver the oppressed from every form of bondage, and see lives positively transformed through active, living faith — one soul, one family, one community at a time.

Our Core Values

The convictions that shape everything we do as a ministry.

Salvation First

We hold the message of the Cross as our foundation — every gathering points people to Christ.

Divine Healing

We minister to the sick with faith, expecting God to heal bodies, minds, and hearts.

Freedom & Deliverance

We pray boldly for the oppressed, believing every yoke can be broken by the power of God.

Lifelong Transformation

We disciple people into lasting change — not just a moment, but a new way of living.

Family & Community

We are a spiritual family — carrying one another's burdens and celebrating one another's wins.

Bold Faith

We step out in faith to reach the unreached, believing God for the impossible.

What We Believe

Our doctrine is rooted in the whole counsel of Scripture.

The Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God for faith and living.

The Godhead

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Salvation by Grace

We believe salvation is a free gift received through faith in the finished work of Jesus Christ.

Divine Healing

We believe healing is provided for in the atonement and is God's will for His people today.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the present-day baptism, gifts, and empowering work of the Holy Spirit.

The Church & Christ's Return

We believe the Church is Christ's body on earth, and we live in expectation of His return.
